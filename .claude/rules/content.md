# Content

## Voice
- English, first person, clear and direct. Short sentences.
- No marketing words ("passionate", "cutting-edge", "rockstar", "leverage").
- Show impact with facts and numbers where possible ("reduced sheet setup from 2 h to 5 min").

## Projects
- `summary`: one sentence, max 160 characters, says what the project does.
- Body uses these headings, in order: `## Problem`, `## Solution`, `## Technical details`, `## Result`.
- Tags: lowercase, from a short shared list (e.g. `revit-api`, `csharp`, `wpf`, `dynamo`, `astro`).

## Experience
- 2–4 highlights per job, each starting with a past-tense verb ("Built", "Automated", "Led").
- Do not invent facts. If information is missing, leave a `TODO` and tell me.
